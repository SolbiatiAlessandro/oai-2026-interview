# Behavioral / mission-fit

OpenAI's behavioral rounds are not throwaway. They eliminate candidates
who pass technicals but fail on mission alignment, collaboration, or
disagreement-handling [S1, S3, S11].

At L5+, the behavioral interview is split in two: a **leadership** round
(usually with a senior manager or director) and a **collaboration** round
(with an IC peer) [S3].

## Format

- **Duration**: 30-45 min per round.
- **Style**: STAR-shaped questions, but with mid-story probing.
- **Number**: 1-2 dedicated rounds + behavioral content baked into the
  recruiter and hiring-manager screens.
- **Mix** [S20]: ~85% Meta-Jedi-style behavioral (key accomplishments,
  career trajectory, conflict resolution, feedback up & down) +
  ~15% OpenAI-specific (mission, AGI risk, trust/safety). Hiring
  manager often runs a tough variant: ~12-15 rapid-fire BQs.

The XFN (cross-functional partner) round is sometimes paired with the
hiring-manager behavioral; see [xfn](xfn.md). The "why OpenAI" answer
gets its own page: [why-openai](../prep/why-openai.md).

## What's graded [S1, S3]

- **Mission alignment**: do you have an articulated, non-generic view
  of OpenAI's AGI / safety mission, and have you acted on similar
  values before?
- **Disagreement and conflict**: tell me about disagreeing with a
  manager or peer — what was the substance, how did you escalate,
  what was the outcome?
- **Prioritization under uncertainty**: how do you decide what to
  work on when goals are shifting?
- **Quality / safety voice**: have you raised a concern that wasn't
  popular?
- **Collaboration with research/engineering boundary**: how do you
  work with a counterpart on the *other* side of the
  research-engineering line? [S3]
- **Mentorship and influence** (L5+): leverage beyond your own
  output [S3].

## Signature questions reported

- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a senior leader. What did
  you do?" [S1, S3]
- "Tell me about a project that failed and what you learned." [S5]
- "Tell me about working with a difficult collaborator." [S3]
- "How do you handle ambiguity or shifting priorities?" [S5]
- "When have you raised a quality / safety concern?" [S1]
- "Why OpenAI?" — non-generic answer required [S1, S5, S13].

## STAR brief

For each prepared story:

- **Situation** (10s): the context.
- **Task** (10s): what you owned.
- **Action** (most of it): what *you* specifically did, including
  trade-offs you weighed.
- **Result** (15s): outcome with concrete numbers and what you learned.

OpenAI panels probe the *Action* heavily — prepare to defend *why*
you picked your action over alternatives [S3].

## Failure modes [S3, S11]

- Generic mission answers.
- Stories with no real tension or trade-off.
- Stories where "we" did the work and you can't disambiguate your
  contribution.
- Failure stories with no genuine learning.
