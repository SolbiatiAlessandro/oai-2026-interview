# XFN (cross-functional partner) round

A behavioral / collaboration round paired with the hiring-manager
behavioral on some loops. Reported in S20: ~30 min, with a non-
engineering counterpart (PM-style), centered on cross-team
collaboration and competing priorities.

## Format

- ~30 min, conversational.
- Two-question shape reported [S20]:
  1. Walk me through an example of working with a PM.
  2. Share an experience competing on priorities with an XFN team.
- Very similar in shape to the hiring-manager behavioral round —
  prepare with the same STAR stories, just biased toward the
  cross-functional axis.

## What's graded

- **Cross-functional collaboration**: do you treat PMs / DSes /
  research counterparts as peers, or as input-providers?
- **Conflict and prioritization**: how do you decide what *not* to
  do when two valuable things are competing?
- **Communication clarity**: can you explain a technical decision
  to a non-engineering stakeholder?
- **Ownership of outcomes**: did the example end with a measurable
  business / product result, or just "we shipped it"?

## Story template for this round

For each of two stories, prepare:

- The XFN partner (PM / DS / designer / research scientist).
- The competing-priority decision.
- Your role *as the engineer* in the negotiation.
- The trade-offs you weighed (impact, effort, risk, time).
- The outcome with concrete numbers.
- What you'd do differently.

## Practice

- Mock with someone who *has been* a PM or manager — peer-mocks with
  other engineers tend to be too lenient on this round [S20].
