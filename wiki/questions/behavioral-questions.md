# Behavioral questions (bank)

Questions reported across OpenAI behavioral / mission-fit rounds [S1, S3,
S5, S11, S13].

## Mission / "why OpenAI"

- Why OpenAI specifically (and not other AI labs)?
- What's your current view on the AGI roadmap?
- Which OpenAI publication / launch did you find most interesting and
  why?
- What's a way OpenAI could fail at its mission, and what would you
  do about it?
- What does "safety" mean to you in practice?

## Disagreement & conflict

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a manager. What was the
  substance? What did you do? What was the outcome?
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a peer about a technical
  decision. How did you resolve it?
- A senior engineer pushes a design you think is risky. Walk me
  through how you handle it.
- Describe a time you escalated something and how you decided to
  escalate vs. work around it.

## Failure / learning

- Tell me about a project that failed. What did you learn?
- Tell me about a decision you made that you'd reverse today.
- What's a piece of feedback that changed how you work?
- Tell me about a bug or incident you caused.

## Prioritization & ambiguity

- How do you decide what to work on when goals are shifting?
- Tell me about working in a problem space without clear success
  metrics.
- Tell me about a time you had to drop work mid-project. How did you
  decide?

## Quality / safety

- When have you raised a quality or safety concern that wasn't
  popular?
- Describe a time you slowed down a launch for the right reason.
- How would you push back on a deadline you think is unsafe to meet?

## Collaboration (engineering ↔ research)

- How do you work with a counterpart on the other side of the
  research-engineering line?
- Tell me about partnering with a researcher on shipping something.
- A researcher hands you experimental code that doesn't run at
  production scale. What do you do?

## Leadership (L5+ rounds)

- Tell me about a time you changed your team's technical direction.
- Tell me about mentoring a struggling engineer.
- Walk me through a multi-quarter initiative you led from concept
  to outcome.
- How do you build technical strategy across teams?

## Closing questions

- What are your biggest strengths? Biggest weaknesses?
- Why are you leaving your current role?
- What questions do you have for us? (Don't no-op. Pre-prepare 3+
  substantive questions.)
